# APAC and Canada Retail Broker Apps: Bottom Tab / Navigation Re-verification (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-18. Budget: about 18 search/fetch calls. Confidence labels:
**Verified** = an official primary source names the tab/label. **Partially verified** = an official source names some labels or describes the feature but not the full bar/order. **Unverified** = no citable source found; not asserted.

Important caveat: for most Japanese apps, the official guide pages I could reach describe the 「メニュー」 screen (a hamburger/menu list), not the bottom tab bar. I did **not** find a citable source for the exact bottom tab labels of SBI 株アプリ, SBI 米国株アプリ, iSPEED, マネックス証券アプリ, 三菱UFJ eスマート証券 (formerly auカブコム), PayPay証券, Syfe/Endowus, CommSec Pocket, or Futu NiuNiu (Chinese-language app). Those labels are listed under Gaps and must not be filled in from memory.

## Q1. Exact bottom tab labels/order per app (with citation, date, confidence)

### Takeaway
Of the apps in scope, only **moomoo (Futu's international app)** has an official source that lists the full bottom-bar order: Watchlist / Markets / Accounts / News / Discover / Me (AU guide). **Wealthsimple** has an official dated (2025-08-18) description of its swipeable Home / Spend / Invest structure plus base-of-app icons. **Tiger Trade** is partially verified: release notes name Watchlist, Portfolio, a Profile tab, and a "我的" menu. Japanese official guides document 「メニュー」 screens rather than tab bars, so the Japanese tab labels stay unverified.

## Q2. Which apps lead with watchlist vs assets vs feed/community?

### Takeaway
moomoo leads with **Watchlist** (tab 1, default page). Wealthsimple leads with a **Home** money/accounts overview. Futu NiuNiu (HK) markets itself on **community plus AI**. Japanese apps (iSPEED My Page, Monex 「資産」 icon, PayPay "すべての資産額" on top) emphasise customizable dashboards and assets, but tab order is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- moomoo: Watchlist is the first tab and the default page. — [moomoo AU overview](https://www.moomoo.com/au/learn/overview)
- Wealthsimple: Home is the center, with Spend and Invest reached by swipe. — [Wealthsimple 2025-08-18](https://product-news.wealthsimple.com/meet-our-re-designed-wealthsimple-app-appui)
- iSPEED: the top screen is a customizable My Page (up to 10 pages). — [App Store JP: iSPEED](https://apps.apple.com/JP/app/id389339704)
- PayPay証券: "すべての資産額" was added to the top screen (Mar 2024, per snippet). — [Impress Watch](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1581210.html)
- 富途牛牛: the app title itself foregrounds 投資社區 and AI投資分析工具. — [App Store: 富途牛牛](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6752377955)

### Inferences
- There is a split: Chinese-origin brokers (Futu/moomoo, Tiger) are watchlist-first with heavy community and feed. The Canadian neobroker Wealthsimple is money-first (unified bank, spend, and invest). Japanese brokers favour dense, customizable dashboards.

### Gaps
- Default landing screens for SBI, Monex, eスマート, Tiger, and Stake are not verified.

## Q3. Where are paper trading and strategy/screener tools?

### Takeaway
moomoo puts **Papertrade inside Discover** (with Moo and Learn), not as a bottom tab, and puts Heat Map, Earnings Calendar, and Institutional Tracking under **Markets**. Tiger offers free **demo accounts** for stocks, options, and futures (2026 release notes added HK single-leg options demo trading), but no tab location is documented. Monex's 銘柄スカウター screener sits under 「メニュー」→マーケット情報. No official SBI or 楽天 paper-trading feature was found.

### Cited Findings
- moomoo: Papertrade is a Discover sub-tab, and the Markets tab holds the analysis tools. — [moomoo AU overview](https://www.moomoo.com/au/learn/overview)
- Tiger: "Complimentary demo accounts for stocks, options and futures". The SG listing also notes strategy-mode closed-position P&L (9.5.8.0, Jun), options analytics/GEX/max pain (9.6.1–9.6.2, Aug–Sep), and TigerAI (report and news summaries, price-move analysis). — [App Store SG: Tiger Trade](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/tiger-trade-invest-globally/id1023600494)
- Per search snippet, Tiger added HK single-leg options Demo Trading. — [App Store: Tiger Trade](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1023600494)
- Monex: 銘柄スカウター is registered from 「メニュー」→「マーケット情報」. — [Monex news 2020-05-08](https://info.monex.co.jp/news/2020/20200508_01.html)
- 富途牛牛: the App Store page does not mention paper trading. — [App Store: 富途牛牛](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6752377955)

### Inferences
- Paper trading is consistently a secondary surface (sub-tab or account switch), never a primary tab, in the apps that have it.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of SBI or 楽天 demo/paper-trading features in their apps. Absence was not confirmed either.
- I did not determine where the screener lives in moomoo (likely Markets) or Tiger.

## Q4. Distinctive patterns

### Takeaway
(1) Futu/moomoo's community (Moo / 牛牛圈) sits in a Discover tab alongside Learn and Papertrade, and both tabs and sub-tabs are user-rearrangeable. (2) Wealthsimple uses swipe-between surfaces (Spend ← Home → Invest) plus base icons (move money, activity, Discover), which fits a unified money app. (3) Japanese brokers use multi-app families with a 「メニュー」 hub, and SBI's 2026 "Plus" app acts as a launcher via 「アプリ一覧」. (4) AI is moving into content surfaces: 牛牛AI in news and community (Aug–Oct), TigerAI, and Stake's MCP server for external AI agents.

### Cited Findings

- Wealthsimple's swipe structure and Discover magnifier. — [Wealthsimple 2025-08-18](https://product-news.wealthsimple.com/meet-our-re-designed-wealthsimple-app-appui)
- SBI Plus 「アプリ一覧」 launches the other apps. — [SBI Plus menu](https://search.sbisec.co.jp/v2/popwin/guide/tool/sbi_app/02_basic/menu.html); [SBI press 2025-12-17](https://search.sbisec.co.jp/v2/popwin/info/home/irpress/prestory251217_011500.pdf)
- 牛牛AI entry points in 資訊/社區/投教. — [App Store: 富途牛牛](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6752377955)
- Stake MCP server. — [Stake support: MCP](https://hellostake.com/au/support/products-and-features/mcp-server/56913247594905)
- iSPEED's pro-trader direction (武蔵 order-from-book, iPad 3-pane). — [Rakuten press 20251121](https://www.rakuten-sec.co.jp/web/company/newsrelease/pdf/press20251121.pdf); [Rakuten press 20251219](https://www.rakuten-sec.co.jp/web/company/newsrelease/pdf/press20251219.pdf)
