# US Incumbent Broker Apps + IBKR + Webull: iOS Bottom Tab Bar Re-verification (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-18. About 22 search/fetch calls. Nothing was checked against the live iOS apps, and App Store screenshots could not be viewed. Schwab (schwab.com, welcome.schwab.com), IBKR Campus GlobalTrader lessons, and BusinessWire returned 403 to the fetch tool, so several official pages could not be read.

Labels used below:
- **Verified**: an official source (help page, release notes, or App Store "What's New") dated 2025–2026 names the tab as a bottom tab.
- **Partially verified**: official but undated or older, or a dated third-party review only, or an official source that names some tabs but not the full set or order.
- **Unverified**: no adequate source.

## Q3. Fidelity Standard vs Trader+, and Webull's current tab bar

### Takeaway
Fidelity runs two IAs inside one app, switched in settings.
- **Standard** is planning- and account-oriented: Home, Investing, Transact, Planning, Discover.
- **Trader+** swaps in trader destinations: Portfolio, Watchlist, Markets in the bottom nav, plus faster trade-ticket access, a compact/heatmap Positions view and an immersive mode.

Webull US centres on Watchlists, Feeds, Markets, Account (center) and Menu, with paper trading under Markets.

### Cited Findings
- Trader+ launched about Sep 2025 ("compact views for faster analysis", based on upvoted customer feedback). The bottom nav gained Portfolio, Watchlist and Markets. Jan 2026: Trader+ is toggled in settings ("Updates to Fidelity Trader+ and standard modes"). Mar 2026: balance restrictions are viewable "in both Standard and Trader+ modes". May 2026: Standard uses "Investing > Positions" while Trader+ uses "Portfolio > Positions", so the same content sits under a different tab name per mode. — [Fidelity What's New](https://fidelity.com/mobile/whats-new-fidelity-app)
- Standard: Home, Investing, Transact, Planning, Discover (review dated 2026-09-30). — [StockBrokers Fidelity](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/fidelityinvestments)
- Webull: see Q1. Official help sources: [order FAQ](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/291-Where-do-I-place-an-order), [Getting Started](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/11032-Getting-Started), [Paper Trading FAQ](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/11069-Paper-Trading).
- Webull 11.0 (official AU learn page, undated): a search bar is pinned at the bottom of Markets, and Feeds combines community, news and Learn. — [Welcome to Webull 11.0](https://www.webull.com.au/learn/courseware/kkChcs/Welcome-to-Webull-11-0)

### Inferences
- In Fidelity's split, "Investing" (Standard) and "Portfolio" (Trader+) are the same object under different names. Planning and Discover (Standard) give way to Watchlist and Markets (Trader+). This is a direct template for a monticker simple/pro toggle.
- Webull's official help still references a top-right magnifying glass, while 11.0 moved search to the bottom of Markets. Help pages may lag the app.

### Gaps
- Fidelity Trader+'s complete tab set (does Transact or Discover survive? is there a 5th tab?) is not documented.
- Webull help pages are undated, and the exact US tab order is unconfirmed.

## Q4. Home content, trade entry, watchlists, search, research/AI, alerts, and paper trading per app

### Takeaway
Paper trading is reachable on mobile in four of these app families:
- **Webull**: Markets > paperTrade.
- **Power E*TRADE**: a "Paper" tab on the login screen, then a yellow banner while in paper mode.
- **thinkorswim**: paperMoney is included in mobile, and the path is reportedly via More.
- **IBKR**: a paper account exists, but no mobile path was found in 2025 notes.

Fidelity has no paper trading (review dated Sep 2026). Trade entry is mostly a tab (E*TRADE, Power E*TRADE, thinkorswim, likely IBKR Mobile) or an action (IBKR GT top tab or button, Fidelity Transact > Trade or symbol search > trade ticket).
