# US Neobroker iOS Navigation Re-verification (Robinhood, Public, SoFi, Cash App, Acorns, Stash), 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-10-18. Method: web search + fetch of official help-center articles, official newsroom/press, official social accounts, and dated third-party reviews. No live app was inspected. App Store screenshots and YouTube transcripts could not be read with available tools.

Label legend:
- **Verified**: official/primary source (help center, newsroom, official social account), with a date where one exists. Many help articles are undated (only a "© 2026" footer), and this is noted.
- **Partially verified**: secondary but specific and dated (e.g., hands-on broker review).
- **Unverified**: no citable source, or only low-quality/conflicting sources.

Builds on the earlier note `research_notes/주식거래 앱 메뉴 구성 비교/us_retail_apps.md`, which covered the Oct 2018 Robinhood tab-bar announcement, Cortex Digests and Portfolio Digests announcements, Public Key Moments/Alpha/Generated Assets, and Fidelity Liquid Glass. Those findings are not repeated except where new evidence changes them.

## Q1. Exact bottom tab labels and order on iOS today, per app

### Takeaway
Even with targeted searching, **no app in scope has a full, official, dated list of its current iOS bottom tabs**. Official help articles confirm only individual tab names: Robinhood "Investing" (or "Investment"), "Crypto", "Home" (Banking), "Account (person icon)", and a newly announced "Predict" tab (Sep 29, 2026). Cash App "Money" tab (bottom-left). SoFi "Invest" (bottom). Public's official help refers only to a top-left ≡ menu on the portfolio page. The full order for every app remains **unverified**.

### Cited Findings

**Robinhood**
- **Verified (official, Dec 2025 + undated help articles): "Account (person icon)" is a top-level destination.** Robinhood support says "Go to Account (person icon) → Menu (3 bars)" (crypto wallet export). — [Robinhood support: Robinhood Earn](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/crypto-earn/) (undated, © 2026). The official @AskRobinhood account gave the same path for notification settings, "Go to Account (person icon) → Select Menu (3 bars) → Settings", in Dec 2025. — [Robinhood Help on X](https://x.com/AskRobinhood/status/1999166314183594430?lang=en). "Link your accounts" and "Your profile" help articles use the same label without stating a screen position. — [Link your accounts](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/link-your-accounts/), [Your profile](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/profile/)
- **Verified (official, undated help): "Investment"/"Investing" and "Crypto" tabs exist.** "go to the **Investment or Crypto tab**, scroll to Earn and select Start earning." — [Robinhood support: Robinhood Earn](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/crypto-earn/). Holdings are under "Investing → Stocks & ETFs" and "Investing → Options". — [Robinhood support: Investing tools](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/investing-tools/)
- **Verified (official, undated): Banking has a "Home" tab.** Direct deposit is set up from "Home tab → Set up direct deposit". — [Robinhood Banking: Get started](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-banking-get-started/). The earlier note also found a separate **Family** tab and Home > Credit in the Banking experience. — [Robinhood Banking support](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-banking/)
- **Verified (official exec announcement, reported Sep 29–30, 2026): a new "Predict" tab.** At HOOD Summit '26 (Sep 29, 2026), Brad Nave (senior director of execution services) announced "a new Predict tab and prediction-market hub", alongside earnings-metric binary contracts rolling out "in the coming weeks". This is reported by MarketBeat via Yahoo Finance. The quote is attributed to Robinhood, but I did not find a Robinhood newsroom post with it. — [Yahoo Finance/MarketBeat, Sep 30 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/robinhood-markets-unveils-24-7-020205692.html). Summit date confirmed by the [Robinhood newsroom, Aug 10 2026](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-presents-hood-summit-26-engines-of-creation/)
- **Partially verified / conflicting (affiliate review, updated Sep 22, 2026):** TheLines lists the bottom bar as "Menu, Search, Markets, Account, and Cash". — [TheLines Robinhood app review](https://www.thelines.com/prediction-markets/robinhood/app/). This conflicts with official help text (Menu is reached *inside* Account, per the official path Account → Menu). It also omits Investing/Crypto/Predict. Treat as **unreliable**.
- **Partially verified (StockBrokers.com hands-on, Oct 1, 2026):** "The home screen stacks every asset you hold as you scroll". Search doubles as a discovery feed with sub-tabs "now, upcoming, macro, crypto, and sports". The Macro tab opens on sector heat maps. Crypto browsing is under "a crypto tab in the app's search". No bottom-bar description. — [StockBrokers.com Robinhood review](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/robinhood)
- Third-party guides place the person/Account icon at the **bottom-right**. Robinhood's own pages do not state the position. — [Investing Simple](https://investingsimple.com/robinhood-hours-contact-support/), [Tradingsim (2026)](https://www.tradingsim.com/blog/day-trading-on-robinhood-pros-and-cons). Partially verified at most.
- **Overall Robinhood tab order: Unverified.** Tab names with official support: Investing/Investment, Crypto, Account (person icon), Predict (new, Sep 2026). There is also a Search destination with sub-tabs (secondary source).

**Public.com**
- **Verified (official help, undated): no bottom-tab names documented.** Official help says to "select the Menu icon ≡ … in the top left corner" of the portfolio page, then Settings. — [Public help: account number](https://help.public.com/en/articles/4375182-where-do-i-find-my-account-number), [Public help: account documents](https://help.public.com/en/articles/5057637-where-do-i-find-my-account-documents), [Public help: app version](https://help.public.com/en/articles/12110586-how-do-i-find-what-app-version-i-m-on). In-app chat lives under "Help Center" in the Settings menu. — [Public: How to reach us](https://public.com/learn/how-to-reach-us)
- **Partially verified (StockBrokers.com, Sep 18, 2026):** a "**Markets tab**" exists with earnings calendars, index performance, Treasuries, a yield curve, major indices, and top movers. — [StockBrokers.com Public review](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/public-com)
- Partially verified (2026 review, generic wording): the main tabs include a portfolio view, a section for exploring assets and other users, and a feed with news, live events, and education. — [Listen Money Matters Public review](https://www.listenmoneymatters.com/public-review/). No exact labels.
- **Overall Public tab order: Unverified.** Only "Markets" (secondary) and a portfolio home with a top-left ≡ menu (official) are evidenced.

**SoFi (Invest within SoFi app)**
- **Verified (official help, undated):** "Open the SoFi Mobile App and tap the '**Invest**' icon at the **bottom of the screen**." — [SoFi support: buy and sell a stock](https://support.sofi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044854191-How-do-I-buy-and-sell-a-stock-on-the-SoFi-app) (quoted via search snippet; direct fetch returned 403). **Cash App (Investing)**

### Inferences
- Robinhood's help text suggests a tab bar organized around **product areas** (Investing, Crypto, Predict, plus Banking's Home/Family in the Banking experience) plus **Search** and **Account**, rather than a classic Watchlist/Markets/Trade bar. Settings and Menu (≡) are nested under Account.
- Cash App and SoFi are super-apps where investing is **one tab (SoFi "Invest") or one tile inside a tab (Cash App Money → Stocks/Bitcoin)**.

### Gaps
- Full order and count for all apps: no official or reliable source. Would need App Store screenshots or a hands-on check. App Store "What's New" for Robinhood is uninformative ("Bug fixes and improvements", per the earlier note).
- YouTube 2025–2026 walkthrough transcripts and Reddit nav-change threads were not retrievable in this pass.
- Public: no help article names any bottom tab.

## Q2. Where are crypto, retirement, options, prediction markets placed in Robinhood navigation?

### Takeaway
Official help puts **Crypto as its own tab** (alongside "Investment/Investing"). **Options** live inside Investing (Investing → Options). **Retirement (IRA)** is accessed by **selecting the account**, which suggests an account switcher rather than a tab. **Prediction markets** moved from a home-screen section/hub to a **new Predict tab** announced Sep 29, 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Crypto tab: "Investment or Crypto tab" — [Robinhood support: Earn](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/crypto-earn/) (Verified, undated). Secondary: crypto discovery is also a sub-tab in Search ("now, upcoming, macro, crypto, and sports"). — [StockBrokers.com, Oct 1 2026](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/robinhood)
- Options: "go to Investing → Options". — [Robinhood support: Investing tools](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/investing-tools/) (Verified, undated)
- Retirement: in the app, "select the account (for example, Traditional IRA) → IRA Settings → Investing". In web classic, "select Retirement → Settings → Investing". In Legend, "select the account icon at the top-right". IRA trading is "Select the applicable IRA account" → "Trade → Buy". Some settings are under "Menu (3 bars) → Investing". — [Robinhood support: Retirement investing](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/retirement-investing/) (Verified, undated)
- Prediction markets: "a new Predict tab and prediction-market hub" announced Sep 29, 2026. — [Yahoo Finance/MarketBeat, Sep 30 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/robinhood-markets-unveils-24-7-020205692.html) (Verified as an exec statement, via press). Before this, StockBrokers.com (Oct 1, 2026) saw "The Prediction Markets section of the home screen shows your open positions". Event contracts are app-only. — [StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/robinhood). A Feb 2026 user post said prediction markets were on the "front page" and could be removed by contacting support. — [X post](https://x.com/TodayIWorkOn/status/2027759605309124771) (anecdotal). Affiliate reviews conflict (some say reached via search or the Investing menu). — [OddsAssist](https://oddsassist.com/prediction-markets/robinhood/), [BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/reviews/robinhood/), [next.io](https://next.io/prediction-markets/robinhood/app/)
- Also announced at HOOD Summit '26: **Robinhood Agents** (agentic accounts, recurring "loops", trade approvals on by default, 150k+ agentic accounts since the May launch), Robinhood Social out of beta, and **Dual Display** redesign for foldables/iPad (Oct 23). — [Yahoo Finance/MarketBeat](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/robinhood-markets-unveils-24-7-020205692.html)

### Inferences
- Robinhood's 2026 structure looks like: product tabs (Investing incl. stocks/ETFs/options; Crypto; Predict), Retirement and multiple accounts via account selection, and a separate Banking experience with its own Home/Family tabs.

### Gaps
- Whether the Predict tab has shipped to all users as of Oct 18, 2026, and its position in the bar: unverified.
- Where Robinhood Agents/agentic accounts and Robinhood Strategies sit in navigation: not found.
- Whether Social has its own tab: not found.

## Q3. How do users reach AI explanations of "why a stock moved"?

### Takeaway
Robinhood: **Cortex Digests on the stock/crypto detail page** and **Portfolio Digests in Investing** (Gold only, not IRAs; rolling release). Public: **Key Moments on the asset chart** and **Alpha** AI summaries. No home-screen-first AI feed is officially documented for either.

### Cited Findings
- Robinhood (Verified, official help, undated): Digests "for assets" appear on a stock's or crypto's detail page. "Portfolio Digests in Investing across account types, except for IRAs", as a "rolling release". Gold-only ($5/mo). Cover the most popular stocks/ETFs and all tradable crypto, not options or futures. — [Robinhood support: Cortex Digests](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cortex-digests)
- Secondary confirmation (Oct 1, 2026): a stock page shows "a Cortex digest, an AI summary of why Apple slipped 0.37%". — [StockBrokers.com](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/robinhood)
- Q1 2026 (Apr 28, 2026): Cortex Digests used by nearly 1M customers. Cortex Assistant is "rolling out across the app". — [Robinhood Q1 2026 results (Seeking Alpha PR)](https://seekingalpha.com/pr/20491386)
- Public: Key Moments shows AI summaries of price moves on the asset chart (official features page, from earlier note). — [Public features](https://public.com/features). Alpha AI summaries help skim market news and the context behind moves (Sep 18, 2026). — [StockBrokers.com Public review](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/public-com). Alpha is also a standalone iOS app (subscription), still free inside Public. — [PR Newswire: Alpha app](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alpha-a-new-ai-watchlist-app-launches-to-give-individual-investors-real-time-insights-on-the-markets-302330059.html), [App Store: Alpha](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6737196011)
- Public Generated Assets is described as a research tool turning prompts into themed indexes. Location on mobile is not specified. Public has **no paper trading** (Sep 2026). — [StockBrokers.com Public review](https://www.stockbrokers.com/review/public-com)

### Inferences
- The pattern is AI explanation **at the asset detail page** plus a **portfolio-level digest inside the investing area**, not a dedicated tab. Robinhood's Cortex Assistant appears to be an app-wide overlay, not a tab.

### Gaps
- No SoFi or Cash App "why it moved" feature found (Cash App Moneybot is a general assistant).
- Exact UI entry point for Cortex Assistant (button? search bar?): not documented.
