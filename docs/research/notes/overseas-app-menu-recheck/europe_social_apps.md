# European and Social Investing Apps: Mobile Bottom Tab Bar Re-verification (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-18. About 45 search and fetch calls.

**Confidence labels used below**
- **Verified:** a primary or official source names the tab or label, and the source is dated or currently live.
- **Partially verified:** an official source names some of the tabs, or a third-party source is dated, or the order is unknown.
- **Unverified:** no citable source was found.

**Overall caveats**
- App Store screenshots cannot be read with these tools.
- revolut.com and help.revolut.com, Bitpanda support, and BrokerChooser returned HTTP 403. Revolut evidence therefore comes from search-engine snippets of Revolut's own help pages, not from fetched pages.
- The Stocktwits help article on its navigation bar returned 404 when fetched. Its content comes from a search snippet.
- No source found gives the full left-to-right tab order for eToro, Revolut, Freetrade, Trade Republic, Bitpanda or Stocktwits.

## Q1. Exact bottom tab labels and order per app (with citation, date, confidence)

### Takeaway
Only moomoo US has an official, ordered six-tab list, and moomoo also documents a customisable "Dock" with a five-tab pinned limit. Trading 212 is the next best documented, with four tabs named in an official help article (Home, Portfolio, Card, Menu/More) plus a central icon. Revolut ("Invest" on the bottom menu), Freetrade (Portfolio, Discover, Activity) and Scalable ("Finden", Profile at bottom right) have one to three labels confirmed each. The tab bars of eToro's rebuilt app, Trade Republic, Bitpanda and Stocktwits remain unverified, although Trade Republic's top-level Wealth/Cash split is confirmed.

### Inferences
- European neobrokers fall into three IA archetypes:
  - **Minimal (Trade Republic):** a section switch plus floating actions.
  - **Compact four- or five-tab bar (Trading 212, Scalable, Freetrade):** a portfolio-first layout with a dedicated search/discover tab and a profile/more tab at the far right.
  - **Super-app (Revolut):** investing is one tab ("Invest") inside a banking app.
- Trading 212 puts **Switch to practice** inside the More tab. eToro places Real/Virtual under the username menu. Neither makes demo trading a tab, which is consistent with the earlier finding that paper trading works as a mode toggle. moomoo is the exception, with Papertrade under the Discover tab.
- moomoo's move to a user-customisable Dock (five pinned tabs plus recent tabs) is a notable alternative to fixed tab bars for apps with many features.

### Gaps
- The bottom tab bar of eToro's rebuilt app (live since Oct 2026) could not be confirmed from any primary source. Checking it needs a device or App Store screenshots.
- The full Revolut main-app tab list, Bitpanda's tabs, and the order of the Freetrade and Stocktwits tabs are unknown.
- The label of Trading 212's centre icon is unknown. Neither T212 help article is dated.
- Whether moomoo regions outside the US use the Dock model was not systematically checked.

## Q2. Did Trading 212 roll back its Dec 2025 redesign? What is the current navigation?

### Takeaway
Yes, the rollback shipped. Trading 212 staff announced on 2025-12-24 that the previous layout would return in January. On 2026-01-12 a staff member posted that "the old new portfolio look is now live" on web, rolling out on mobile in v8.14.1. The rollback was partial: users kept asking for Y-axis values and pie/holding filters into April 2026. The redesign affected the **Portfolio** screen, not the tab bar.

### Cited Findings
- **2025-12-24, KrisG** (T212 "What's new" category): "we'll be rolling out an update in January that brings back the previous layout." — [T212 Community: Portfolio Redesign Update](https://community.trading212.com/t/portfolio-redesign-update/89501)
- **2026-01-12, KrisG:** "the update is now live on the web app and rolling out on mobile with version 8.14.1." — [T212 Community: The old new portfolio look is now live!](https://community.trading212.com/t/the-old-new-portfolio-look-is-now-live/89758)
- **User replies (Jan–Apr 2026)** confirm several things returned: the original look, removal of the pull-up assets bar, and pies "back where they belong as part of investments". Other things did not return:
  - Y-axis values on the portfolio and pie charts (still missing on 2026-01-27)
  - filtering pies versus individual investments (still missing on 2026-04-02)

  — [same thread](https://community.trading212.com/t/the-old-new-portfolio-look-is-now-live/89758)
- **What the redesign was:** bubble-style cards and pull-up menus, holdings hidden behind a search bar, Y-axis values removed from the graph, and the colour theme ignored. Users threatened to switch brokers. — [Coinspectator 2025-12-29](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2025/12/29/trading-212-reverses-interface-redesign-after-users-warn-theyll-switch-brokers-its-like-youre-intentionally-trying-to-anger-us/); [T212 Community: New layout ruined T212](https://community.trading212.com/t/new-layout-ruined-t212/89220)
- **Separate, non-controversial change, 2025-09-23 (staff, Momchil.G):** "We have revamped the home screen." The Home tab now shows total account value, a widgets section (investments, pots, ISA allowance) and a watchlist. Users raised two points:
  - some asked whether anyone lands on Home at all, since their app opens to pies and investments
  - some asked for privacy mode, which Trading 212 shipped as "Introducing Privacy Mode" in 2026

  — [T212 Community: Your home screen just got a new look!](https://community.trading212.com/t/your-home-screen-just-got-a-new-look/87886); [T212 What's new index](https://community.trading212.com/c/whats-new/13)
- **Other 2026 additions:**
  - an in-app AI chatbot, announced 2026-04-03; it "lives right inside the app" and its entry point is not specified
  - AI Analysis in Pies, reported by users in June 2026

  — [T212 Community: Introducing the new AI Chatbot](https://community.trading212.com/t/introducing-the-new-ai-chatbot/91148)
- **Release notes:** German App Store release notes for v8.34–8.51 (Jun–Oct 2026) contain only generic text ("Verbesserungen der Benutzerfreundlichkeit"), so they give no evidence of navigation changes. — [App Store DE: Trading 212](https://apps.apple.com/DE/app/id566325832)
- **Current navigation (Partially verified):** Home · Portfolio · central icon · Card · ☰ More. The More tab contains Switch to practice, Learn/Community forum, Market tools and Support. — [T212 Help: switch accounts](https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/8614634278685-How-do-I-switch-between-my-accounts); [T212 Help: More tab](https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/38653258622493-Introducing-the-new-More-tab-design)

### Inferences
- Trading 212 reversed the redesign in about 19 days, from launch in mid-Dec 2025 to v8.14.1 on 2026-01-12. That is a strong signal that users of active investing apps treat holdings density and chart axes as non-negotiable. Hiding holdings behind search or cards triggers churn threats.
- Most of the community forum moved into "More > Learn". Social features are de-emphasised in Trading 212's navigation.

### Gaps
- The first release date of the Dec 2025 redesign was not pinned down from an official post.
- No source confirms that the Y-axis values were ever restored.

## Q4. How do Trade Republic and Scalable structure a minimal tab bar?

### Takeaway
Trade Republic is the most minimal. It has no conventional multi-tab bar in the evidence:
- two top-level sections, **Wealth** and **Cash** (formerly Portfolio/Cash)
- the profile as an **avatar at top left**
- context-specific floating buttons at the bottom: Transfer at bottom right, "Handeln" (trade) at bottom right on asset pages, "Sparplan" at bottom left
- search reached from the start page

Scalable uses a short bottom bar: a portfolio/home landing, a dedicated **"Finden"** (find/discover) tab, and **Profile at the bottom right**. Since 2022 it has consolidated (crypto folded into Broker). Payments and savings plans live on the home screen rather than in tabs.

## Q5. Where do social feed / copy trading, watchlist, search, news/AI, and paper trading live?

### Takeaway
Each feature has a consistent home across these apps. Social and copy trading live in Discover/Feed surfaces (eToro, moomoo Moo, Stocktwits Home and Trending). Trading 212 de-emphasises social by moving its forum link under More > Learn. The watchlist sits on the portfolio or home screen (Freetrade, Trading 212 Home, Stocktwits Home) or is a tab of its own (moomoo). Paper trading is a mode toggle (Trading 212 "Switch to practice" in More; eToro Real/Virtual), except at moomoo, where it is under Discover. AI is an overlay or chatbot (eToro Tori, the Trading 212 AI chatbot) rather than a tab.
